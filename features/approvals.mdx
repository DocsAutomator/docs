With Approvals, a teammate checks each document before it goes out. DocsAutomator makes the document as usual, then holds it until your approvers approve it. Nothing is sent, saved or signed before that.

Approvals work for every document run:

- Google Doc, Word and PDF templates.
- Documents you create by hand in the app, from a data source trigger, through the API, through the MCP server or with the DocsAutomator Agent.
- Plain document runs, and runs with [signing or acceptance](/features/docsautomator-esign).

<Note>
Approvals are available on the current Business and Scale plans. See [Plans](#plans).
</Note>

## Set up approvals

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/approvals/approvals-card.png" style={{ maxWidth: "360px", margin: "0 auto" }} alt="The Approvals card of an automation. The toggle Approve each document before it goes out is on. Under Approvers, two of four team members are checked. The Rule is All approvers must approve. Under When approval is needed, Only when is selected with the condition amount is greater than 5000." />
</Frame>

1. Open the automation and click the **Approvals** card.
2. Switch on **Approve each document before it goes out**.
3. Under **Approvers**, select one or more members of your team.
4. If you select more than one approver, choose a **Rule**:
   - **All approvers must approve:** every approver must approve. One rejection stops the document.
   - **Any one approver is enough:** the first decision counts, approve or reject.
5. Under **When approval is needed**, choose **Always** or **Only when...**.

### Approve only some documents

With **Only when...**, a document waits for approval only when the condition is true. All other documents go out at once, as without approval. A condition uses the same rules as [Sections](/features/sections): pick a field or placeholder, a comparison and a value. You can add more than one condition and choose whether all or any of them must be true.

The condition reads values in this way:

- Amounts such as `$5,000.00` count as numbers. A value such as `5,000` or `5.000` is read with the automation's language and region.
- Dates must be in the form `YYYY-MM-DD`, for example `2026-10-31`.
- If a condition cannot be checked, the document waits for approval. For example, this happens when the value is empty or is not a number.

The review page shows why a document needs approval, for example "Needs approval because amount is 7,400 (rule: amount is greater than 5,000)".

## Approvers from your data

Often the right approver changes from record to record, for example the person who requested a contract. For Airtable, Notion, Google Sheets, SmartSuite, ClickUp and Google Forms, add one or more fields under **Approvers**, in **From your data**, whose value holds the email of the approver. Each document then also asks the people in these fields. For the API and other tools that send data, see [Approvers in an API call](#approvers-in-an-api-call).

1. Under **From your data**, click **Add a field** and pick the field, for example **Requested by**. Click **Add another field** for a second field, for example **Legal reviewer**.
2. Under **Allowed email domains**, add the domains of the people who may join to approve, for example `acme.com`. While the list is empty, nobody outside your team joins, and the owners approve instead of people who are not on your team.

A field can hold one email or several, separated by commas, semicolons or line breaks. People fields, collaborator fields and lookups of email fields work too. Each document can have up to 10 approvers from your data.

The **Rule** applies to all approvers together: the team members you selected plus every person from the fields. With **All approvers must approve**, the document waits until each of them approves.

### People who are not on your team yet

A person who is not on your team can approve only when both of these are true:

- Their email is on one of the **Allowed email domains** in the Approvals card. Team members can always approve, whatever their domain.
- The person joins your workspace as an approver.

The list of allowed email domains is your consent. Under the list, the card says **People on these domains can join as approvers**, with who added the last domain and when. Only owners and admins can change the fields and the domains.

The approval email asks the person to join your workspace. Before they join, the review link shows only your workspace name, the automation, who created the document and a **Join** box. It shows no document and no record data. The person sets a password, or uses Google or Microsoft, and joins as an approver. A person who already has a DocsAutomator account logs in and joins with one click. Then the same page shows the document, and they approve or reject.

### The Approver role

A person who joins from an approval email gets the **Approver** role. An approver sees only the **Approvals** page with the documents they approve, and their own account settings. They cannot see automations, documents, signing sessions, templates, the team, billing, data sources or the API key, and they cannot use the AI agent or connect Claude or ChatGPT to the workspace. A link to any other page opens the Approvals page with a note.

Approvers do not cost anything. DocsAutomator charges for documents, not for people.

Owners and admins can change an approver to a member, and a member back to an approver, on **Settings**, **Team**.

### When DocsAutomator cannot use a field

In these cases nobody from your data or the request approves. The workspace owners approve the document instead, together with the team members you selected. Nothing goes out before an approval in any of these cases.

The owners see the reason on the review page, on the **Approvals** page, on the **Documents** page and on the run's own page, with a **Change approvers** link to the automation's Approvals card:

| Case | What the owners see |
|---|---|
| The person is not on your team, and **Allowed email domains** is empty | Owners approve: jane@acme.com is not on the team, and no allowed email domain is set. |
| The person is not on your team, and their domain is not under **Allowed email domains** | Owners approve: jane@gmail.com is not on the team and not on an allowed domain. |
| A value in the field is not an email | Owners approve: "Requested by" has no valid email. |
| The fields or the request hold more than 10 emails | Owners approve: "Requested by" holds more than 10 emails (12). |
| The fields are empty and no team member is selected | Owners approve: "Requested by" is empty. |

When the fields are empty and you selected team members, those team members approve as usual. That is not a refusal.

### Approvers in an API call

For the API and other tools that send data, such as Zapier, Make, n8n and Glide, each request can name approvers for its document. Send their emails in the `approverEmails` key at the top level of the request, next to `data`:

```json
{
  "automationId": "6710f2c4e1a2b3c4d5e6f700",
  "approverEmails": ["dana@acme.com", "leo@acme.com"],
  "data": {
    "client_name": "Northwind Ltd"
  }
}
```

- `approverEmails` can hold one email, several separated by commas, semicolons or line breaks, or a list. Up to 10 people per document.
- DocsAutomator reads `approverEmails` only at the top level of the request. Inside `data` it is an ordinary value and asks nobody. In Zapier and Make, add it as its own field next to the data, not as a placeholder value.
- A request with `approverEmails` waits for approval, even when approval is off for the automation. When approval is on, the approvers you selected in the Approvals card approve too, with the rule you chose. When approval is off, only the people in the request approve, and all of them must approve.
- An empty `approverEmails` still holds the document. The workspace owners approve it.
- Members of your team always count as approvers. Other people can join, as approvers, only when their domain is under the allowed email domains in the Approvals card. Otherwise the owners approve instead.
- On a plan without Approvals, a request with `approverEmails` fails with an error, so a document never goes out without the approval you asked for.

You cannot change the approval settings through the API or an AI agent. The automation's settings in the API and in the [MCP server](/integrations-api/docsautomator-mcp) show them read-only.

## What waits until the approval

While a document waits, DocsAutomator makes and stores the document, but these steps do not run:

- The email with the document.
- The signing or acceptance request.
- The webhook.
- Saving the document to the record in your data source.
- Saving to Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.
- The public link to the file.

When the approval is complete, all of these steps run once, exactly as they would without approval. The delivered document and the data saved to the record are the version that the approvers saw.

<Warning>
If the automation keeps the Google Doc, that Google Doc is made in your Google Drive at once, before the approval. DocsAutomator builds the PDF from it.
</Warning>

Approval also applies in test mode. After the approval, a test document goes out to its real recipients, the same as any other document.

## Review a document

Each approver gets an email with a **Review document** link. Every approver must log in to DocsAutomator to see the document or to decide, with the account the email was sent to. Without a login, the link opens the login page and comes back to the document after the login, also with Google or Microsoft. If you are logged in with another account, the page says **This link is for** followed by the start of the approver's email, and offers **Switch account**. It shows no document data.

The document also waits on the **Approvals** page in the app, under **Waiting for me** and **All pending**. The **Approvals** item in the sidebar shows how many documents wait for you.

The review page shows:

- The PDF, exactly as it goes out.
- **Goes to**: the steps that run after the approval, for example email and webhook.
- Each approver and their decision.

Opening the link does not decide anything. To decide, click **Approve** or **Reject**:

- **Approve:** your approval is recorded. When it completes the rule, the button reads **Approve and send**, and the document goes out at once.
- **Reject:** you must give a reason. Nothing goes out. The person who created the document gets the reason by email. If an automation, the API or another tool created the document, the workspace owners get the email.

To change a document, reject it and create it again.

### Status in the app

On the **Documents** page and in the run history, a held document shows one of these states:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Waiting for approval | The document is made. Nothing went out yet. |
| Rejected | An approver rejected the document. Nothing went out. |
| Expired | Nobody approved the document in time. Nothing went out. |

After the approval, the document shows as a normal finished run. The filters on the **Documents** page include these states.

### Good to know

- Owners and admins can decide for a named approver from the **All pending** tab, for example when the approver is away. The review shows who decided for whom.
- DocsAutomator takes the approvers and the rule from the automation when it creates the document. A later change applies to new documents only.
- If none of the selected approvers is still in the team, the workspace owners must approve.
- A document that nobody approves within 30 days expires, and nothing goes out. For signing and acceptance, the request expires at the end of its link expiration.
- AI agents, the API and the MCP server can create documents that wait for approval. Only people can approve them.

## Signing and acceptance

For an automation with [signing or acceptance](/features/docsautomator-esign), the approval comes before the request. The invitations, the signing links and the write-back of the links to your record wait until the approval. After the approval, the request goes out as usual, and the link expiration starts again, so recipients get the full number of days.

## API and MCP

When a document waits for approval, the [createDocument](/api-reference/documents/create-from-api) call answers at once:

```json
{
  "message": "awaiting_approval",
  "status": "awaiting_approval",
  "logId": "6710f2c4e1a2b3c4d5e6f701",
  "holdId": "6710f2c4e1a2b3c4d5e6f702",
  "documentName": "Invoice INV-1042",
  "pdfUrl": null,
  "googleDocUrl": null,
  "approval": {
    "status": "pending",
    "rule": "all",
    "approvers": [
      { "name": "Priya Nair", "email": "priya@example.com" },
      { "name": "Leo Rossi", "email": "leo@example.com" }
    ],
    "condition": null
  }
}
```

- `pdfUrl` and `googleDocUrl` are `null`. The save fields for Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox and the Word file are not in the answer.
- `approval.condition` shows the result of the condition check, or `null` when the automation uses **Always**.
- With an asynchronous call, `GET /job/:jobId` returns the same body as the job result.
- For a signing or acceptance run, the answer has `signingStatus: "awaiting_approval"` and the same `approval` block, and no signing links.

In the MCP server and the DocsAutomator Agent, the AI tool says that the document waits for approval and names the approvers. It cannot approve the document.

### Webhooks

- **After the approval:** the automation's normal webhook fires once, with the usual body plus an `approval` block.
- **After a rejection:** the webhook URL gets one request with `"event": "document.approval_rejected"`, `"status": "approval_rejected"`, `logId`, `documentName` and an `approval` block with the `reason`.
- No webhook fires when a document starts to wait or when it expires.

Signing and acceptance runs fire their own events: `esign.approval_requested`, `esign.approval_approved` and `esign.approval_rejected`. The payloads are in the [e-sign API reference](/features/docsautomator-esign/docsautomator-esign-api-reference#approval-events).

## Plans

- Approvals are included in the current Business and Scale plans.
- On a trial, the Professional plan and legacy plans, the Approvals card is locked.
- If your workspace moves to a plan without Approvals, the toggle switches off. Your approvers, rule and condition stay saved. Documents that already wait keep waiting until an approver decides. After an upgrade, switch the toggle on again.

Each held document counts as one document when DocsAutomator makes it, the same as any other document. An approval or a rejection does not count it again.
