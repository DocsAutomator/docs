Folders let you group related automations together, making it easier to find and manage them as your workspace grows.

## Creating Folders

1. On the Automations page, click the green **New** button and choose **New folder**
2. Enter a name for your folder (e.g., "Client Invoices", "HR Documents", "Marketing")
3. The folder appears as a row in the automations table, with a count of the automations inside

Double-click a folder row to open it and see its automations.

## Moving Automations to Folders

### Drag and drop

Drag an automation row onto a folder row to move it into that folder.

### Using the selection bar

Click one or more automations to select them (shift-click selects a range), then click **Move** in the selection bar and pick the target folder. When you are inside a folder, the same Move dialog offers **All automations** to move the selection back to the top level.

## Managing Folders

### Renaming

Hover over a folder row, click the **⋮** (Folder actions) menu, and select **Rename**.

### Deleting

Hover over a folder row, click the **⋮** (Folder actions) menu, and select **Delete folder**. When you delete a folder, the automations inside it are not deleted. They are moved back to the top level.

<Warning>
Deleting a folder cannot be undone, but your automations are preserved.
</Warning>

## Tips

- Use folders to separate automations by client, department, or document type
- Each folder row shows a count of the automations inside
- Each automation can only belong to one folder at a time
- Automations without a folder appear at the top level of the automations table
