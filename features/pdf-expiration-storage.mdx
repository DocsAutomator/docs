DocsAutomator allows you to configure how long generated PDFs are retained in the platform's storage system.

## Default Behavior

<Note>
By default, DocsAutomator keeps generated PDFs until you delete them. You can set each automation to delete its PDFs automatically, from about 5 minutes up to 1 year after they are created.
</Note>

## When Storage Matters

For most integrations, such as Airtable and Glide, DocsAutomator places a copy of the generated PDF in your connected data source right after it is created. If you only need that copy, you can set a short expiry.

## Adjusting Retention Settings

To change how long PDFs are kept, change the expiration setting on each automation in the automation settings.

### Available Options

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| No expiration | PDFs kept until you delete them (default) |
| Immediate | PDFs deleted about 5 minutes after generation |
| 1 Day | PDFs retained for 24 hours |
| 3 Days | PDFs retained for three days |
| 7 Days (1 Week) | PDFs retained for one week |
| 14 Days (2 Weeks) | PDFs retained for two weeks |
| 30 Days (1 Month) | PDFs retained for one month |
| 3 Months | PDFs retained for three months |
| 6 Months | PDFs retained for six months |
| 1 Year | PDFs retained for one year |

## Configuration Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Automation Settings">
    Navigate to your automation's settings
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find PDF Settings">
    Locate the "PDF Expiration" option
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Duration">
    Choose your preferred retention period
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save your automation settings
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
For [eSign-enabled automations](/features/docsautomator-esign), PDF expiration is automatically set to "No expiration", so the signed copy stays available as proof. Signing and acceptance records and audit trails are kept for 10 years after the signing session.
</Info>
